## Why public WHOIS is not enough

The registrant name is hidden by privacy protection. A payment provider asking for "proof of ownership" needs the **underlying registration record** held by the registrar (Name.com) or by Lovable as the purchasing party. That record is not visible in public lookups.

## How to get the official document

Because the domain was purchased through Lovable, the registrar account is managed by Lovable. You cannot log into Name.com directly. Request the document from Lovable support.

## What to do while waiting

1. Check **Project Settings → Project section → Domains** in Lovable. Open the three-dot menu on `speakbusy.com` → **Configure** and look for a receipt, invoice, or "Manage domain" option.
2. Screenshot the Lovable Domains page showing `speakbusy.com` as active and connected to your project. Some payment providers accept this as supplementary evidence.
3. Keep the RDAP/WHOIS printout above as supporting documentation.
